# Local Directory Niche Validation

Use this when a user wants to turn a broad local-directory idea into a viable niche/MVP shortlist.

## Pattern

1. **Start from unfair advantages, not only search volume.**
   - Existing business adjacency (e.g. Femme Events → venues/private rooms/weddings).
   - Personal network or domain context (e.g. auto, fitness, local creator/community access).
   - Ability to curate better than generic directories.

2. **Run a wide first-pass search scan across adjacent niches.**
   Search 8-15 candidate angles quickly before narrowing. Good query shape:
   - `<city> <niche> directory`
   - `<city> <niche> guide`
   - `<city> <buyer intent phrase>`
   - `<city> <social/community niche> Instagram Reddit`

3. **Record search-surface signals, not just URLs.**
   Useful signals:
   - Are results mostly generic platforms (Yelp, OpenTable, The Knot, PartySlate, Reddit, Eventbrite)?
   - Are the best answers fragmented across blogs/social/direct business pages?
   - Are there high-intent modifiers (prices, private rooms, micro, near me, beginner-friendly, guest count, neighborhood)?
   - Are operators likely to pay for visibility or leads?
   - Is the data evergreen or constantly changing?

4. **Score candidates with a simple rubric.**
   - Search demand proxy
   - Fragmentation / poor existing UX
   - Unfair advantage
   - Monetization path
   - Maintenance burden
   - Differentiation / editorial voice

5. **Prefer a wedge that can expand sideways.**
   Example from the Atlanta directory session: “Atlanta Private Rooms” beats a pure wedding directory because it captures private dining, rehearsal dinners, bridal showers, birthdays, corporate dinners, date nights, and micro-events while still overlapping Femme Events.

## Output shape

Give the user:
- A ranked shortlist (top 3-7).
- Why each could work.
- The main weakness/risk.
- One recommended first wedge.
- A next validation plan: deeper keyword scan, competitor capture, seed listing count, and 10 monetization/outreach targets.

## Pitfalls

- Do not prematurely build after one exciting idea. Run a fast wide scan first.
- Do not lead with AdSense as the business model; early directory monetization is usually featured listings, lead-gen, sponsored guides, affiliate bookings, or newsletter sponsorships.
- Do not choose a niche only because it has demand; choose one where the user can credibly curate or distribute better than generic platforms.
- For fresh/event-calendar niches, explicitly account for maintenance burden before recommending them.
