# OSP Marketing Copywriter

Based on [Open Strategy Partners](https://openstrategypartners.com) writing methodology.

Core values: **Empathy, Clarity, Trust**

## When to load this skill

- Writing new website copy (service pages, about pages, landing pages)
- Drafting pitch deck narratives or proposal language
- Creating email campaigns or outreach sequences
- Writing social media content calendars
- Generating client deliverable copy (for Papi AI Consulting)
- When Karan says "write copy for..." or "draft a..." for business content

## Content creation protocol

### 1. Research & preparation
Before writing anything:
- **Define target audience** — who specifically is reading this?
- **Identify key message** — what's the ONE thing they should remember?
- **Gather evidence** — testimonials, stats, case studies, specifics
- **Check value map** — does a value map exist for this business? Load it.
- **Note brand voice** — load `femme-events-design` for Femme, match client tone for consulting

### 2. Structure & draft
- Start with thesis/main point (LEDE principle)
- Support with logical evidence chain
- Progress from known → unknown
- End with clear CTA
- Write fast, edit later

### 3. Polish & optimize
- Apply editing codes from `osp-content-editor`
- Check SEO from `osp-seo-optimizer`
- Verify all claims have evidence
- Remove filler and redundancy
- Read aloud for rhythm

## Writing principles

### Voice & tone
- **Active voice** always — "We coordinate your vendors" not "Your vendors are coordinated"
- **Direct address** — "You deserve a stress-free wedding day" not "Couples deserve..."
- **Specific over vague** — "200+ weddings coordinated" not "years of experience"
- **Benefit-first** — lead with outcomes, features follow
- **Sentence case** for all headers — "Our services" not "Our Services"

### Emotional resonance (especially for Femme Events)
- Paint sensory pictures — "candlelit reception under draped linens"
- Use emotional anchors — "the moment you walk down the aisle"
- Acknowledge the stress — then show how you dissolve it
- Aspirational but attainable — luxury doesn't mean unaffordable

### Professional authority (especially for Papi AI Consulting)
- Lead with the client's pain, not your credentials
- Quantify everything — "reduces processing time by 40%" not "streamlines operations"
- Use case studies as proof points
- Technical enough to build trust, simple enough for decision-makers

## Content type templates

### Service page (Femme Events)
```
[Emotional hook — paint the moment]
[Problem statement — what couples struggle with]
[Your approach — how Femme handles it]
[Service tiers — what's included]
[Social proof — testimonial or stat]
[CTA — "Start planning your day"]
```

### Service page (Consulting client)
```
[Problem statement — quantify the pain]
[Your solution — what you deliver]
[How it works — 3-step process]
[Results — metrics and case studies]
[Who it's for — qualifying statement]
[CTA — "Book a discovery call"]
```

### About page
```
[Origin story — why this exists]
[Mission/vision — what you believe]
[Team — humans behind the brand]
[Values — what guides decisions]
[Differentiator — why you, not competitors]
[CTA — connect with us]
```

### Email outreach (cold)
```
Subject: [Under 60 chars, benefit-led, no spam triggers]
[1-line personalized opener — show you know them]
[1-paragraph value prop — what you do + why it matters to THEM]
[1-sentence proof point — stat, case study, or credibility signal]
[Soft CTA — question, not "schedule a call"]
[Signature — name, title, one link max]
Keep total under 150 words.
```

### Email nurture sequence
```
Email 1 (Day 0): Value-first content — helpful resource, no pitch
Email 2 (Day 3): Story/case study — show, don't tell
Email 3 (Day 7): Direct offer — clear CTA with deadline or scarcity
```

### Social media (Instagram for Femme Events)
```
Hook: [Attention-grabbing first line]
Body: [1-3 short paragraphs, sensory language]
CTA: [Engagement prompt or link in bio reference]
Hashtags: [5-10 relevant, mix of broad + niche]
```

### Pitch deck narrative
```
Slide 1: [Client's problem — in their words]
Slide 2: [Cost of inaction — what happens if nothing changes]
Slide 3: [Your approach — how you solve it]
Slide 4: [Evidence — case studies, testimonials, metrics]
Slide 5: [What they get — clear deliverables]
Slide 6: [Investment — pricing with value framing]
Slide 7: [Next steps — clear CTA]
```

## Brand voice reference

### Femme Events voice
- Warm but sophisticated
- Emotionally intelligent
- "We get it" not "We're the best"
- Sensory and visual language
- Confidence without arrogance
- Partnership tone (with Amanda), not corporate

### Papi AI Consulting voice
- Direct and knowledgeable
- Practical over theoretical
- "Here's how" not "Here's why"
- Tech-literate but accessible
- Results-obsessed
- Karan's natural tone: casual but polished

## Quality checklist before delivery

- [ ] Opens with the reader's problem or aspiration (not your business)
- [ ] Every claim is backed by evidence or removed
- [ ] Active voice throughout
- [ ] One clear CTA per piece
- [ ] Reads well on mobile (short paragraphs, scannable)
- [ ] Brand voice is consistent
- [ ] No jargon without definition
- [ ] No clichés ("best in class", "world-class", "cutting-edge")
- [ ] Proofread for grammar and typos
