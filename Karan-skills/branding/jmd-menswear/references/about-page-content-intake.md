# JMD About Page Content Intake

Use this when Karan asks for client-facing content collection for a dedicated JMD About page or owner/story page.

## Purpose
The About page should collect Lucky/Danny's real words and turn them into SEO-safe website copy. The goal is local trust + search relevance, not generic agency storytelling.

SEO lens:
- Branded/local searches: `JMD Menswear`, `JMD Menswear Conyers`, `men's formalwear store Conyers GA`, `local suit store Conyers`.
- Service/event context: suits, tuxedos, formalwear, prom, weddings, quinceañeras, special events.
- E-E-A-T signals: real owners, real store photo, local context, showroom experience, authentic customer approach.

## Client Email Shape
Keep the email easy for Lucky to answer. Do not ask him to write polished copy. Ask structured questions and offer fill-in-the-blank options.

Recommended sections:
1. Photo request
   - Ask for 1–3 real photos of Lucky and Danny inside the showroom.
   - Prefer wide/horizontal, good lighting, store visible in background.
   - State that only approved real photos will be used; no stock/AI images.

2. Who Lucky and Danny are
   - Ask how they describe each person's role.
   - Ask what each cares about when helping a customer.
   - Ask how they would explain who runs JMD.

3. What JMD is about
   - Ask what JMD Menswear is in their own words.
   - Ask what makes JMD different from a regular suit store or online store.
   - Ask what customers appreciate after visiting.
   - Ask what customers should feel walking in.

4. Showroom experience
   - Ask why coming in and trying things on matters.
   - Ask how they help customers choose the right look.
   - Ask which events customers come in for: prom, weddings, quinceañeras, church events, banquets, interviews, photoshoots, date nights, galas, other.
   - Ask what first-time customers should know before visiting.

5. Style and limited-floor language
   - Ask how they describe the pieces they carry.
   - Ask whether “limited” or “one-of-a-kind” is fair wording.
   - Ask for phrases Lucky already says, e.g. “If you like it, come try it on,” “When it’s gone, it’s gone,” “Come see what’s on the floor.”

6. Local connection
   - Ask what they like about serving Conyers/surrounding areas.
   - Ask where customers tend to come from, if they know.
   - Ask whether families/customers return for multiple events.
   - Ask for one anonymized customer story that shows what JMD is about.

7. Boundaries
   - Ask what not to claim: services, pricing, timelines, appointments, inventory, rental workflow, etc.

8. Tone selection
   - Offer simple options: Premium/polished, local/personal, bold/stylish, family/business story, mix.

9. Fill-in shortcut
   - Include: “JMD Menswear is a ____ in Conyers, GA. We help customers find ____ for events like ____. What makes us different is ____. When someone comes into the store, we want them to feel ____. Lucky and Danny care about ____. JMD is all about ____.”

## Copy and Safety Rules
- Everything Lucky provides is source material, not automatically final website copy.
- Final website copy still needs Karan/Lucky approval before public use.
- Preserve Lucky's voice; clean up structure and SEO without turning it into AI-sounding copy.
- Do not invent dates, history, awards, years in business, founder details, pricing, inventory status, or service claims.
- Danny/Karan approval is required for anything touching availability, e-commerce, rentals workflow, pricing, appointments, or purchasing expectations.
- Use real approved JMD photos only. No stock photos. No AI images.

## Good Closing
“Once you send this back, I’ll turn it into clean website copy for the About page and send it back for approval before anything goes live.”
